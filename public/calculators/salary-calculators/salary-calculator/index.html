Optional extra payments

Overtime is added to your gross salary and taxed as ordinary income.

A bonus is added to gross and taxed as ordinary income. Bonus-specific withholding rules (for example the US 22% supplemental rate) are not modelled.

Commission is added to gross and taxed as ordinary income.
