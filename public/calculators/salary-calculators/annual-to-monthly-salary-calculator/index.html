Calculation basis

Your amount is converted to an annual figure first, then: monthly = annual / 12, biweekly = annual / 26, weekly = annual / weeks per year, daily = weekly / workdays per week, and hourly = weekly / hours per week.

How we calculate this

Gross pay only. The weekly, daily and hourly figures use your own hours, weeks and workdays rather than hidden payroll assumptions. Tax, deductions and overtime are not included.
